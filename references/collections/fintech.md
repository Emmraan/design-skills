# Collection: Fintech

Fintech is a trust game: buyers hand over money and data, so the design must feel
**institutional and precise** while still selling the human outcome. This collection maps
the repo's fintech-grade references and the trust mechanics that make them convert.

## Category definition

Banking, payments, investing, insurance, accounting, lending. The product moves money —
credibility and transparency are the primary selling points. See
`patterns/fintech.md` for assembly.

from the third. Fintech is where restraint (not decoration) sells.

## Synthesis guidance

- Front-load trust: an early trust band (insurance, security, compliance) answers the first
  objection before it's asked.
- Transparent fees/pricing is a differentiator — most competitors hide it.
- Use tabular numbers and precise copy; sloppy layout reads as sloppy money handling.
- Keep motion restrained — financial UI should feel deterministic, not playful.
