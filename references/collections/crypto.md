# Collection: Crypto

Crypto design must convert **skeptics, not degens**. Buyers arrive with fraud
anxiety, so the page borrows fintech trust mechanics (legibility, compliance,
restraint) while keeping product-led warmth. Regulation displayed beats
regulation buried.

## Category definition

Exchanges, wallets, on-ramps, staking, crypto derivatives. Goal: trust (compliance,
security, clarity) → first action (signup, field + pill) → trade. The conversion
unit is a signup field beside one pill button — never break that adjacency.

Coinbase is the seed crypto reference; pair it with **fintech references**
(FundingPips for number-hero energy, Ramp for institutional restraint) and
**SaaS patterns** for structure. Contrast with FundingPips deliberately: neutral
shadows vs blue glow, restraint vs adrenaline.

## Synthesis guidance

- Lead with a statement hero and an adjacent field + pill conversion unit.
- Make compliance a visible trust band (regional info, disclosures), not footer fine print.
- Keep body text AAA; relegate muted tones to large or decorative roles.
- Elevate with neutral shadows; reserve glow/color effects for at most one moment.
