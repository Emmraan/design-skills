# Collection: Portfolio

Portfolio design is personal: it shows an individual's (or small team's) taste, craft, and
range. The page must feel like the person, not like a template. This collection maps the
repo's references that model personal, editorial presentation worth synthesizing from.

## Category definition

Personal sites, individual designers/developers, small-studio portfolios. Goal: express
identity + demonstrate skill → get hired/contacted. See `patterns/portfolio-agency.md`
for assembly (it covers both agency and personal).

## Repo references (analyzed in `references/websites/`)

| Site | Slug | What to borrow as logic |
|---|---|---|
| Primora | `primora` | Premium craft presentation; identity-forward art direction |

Portfolio is the most personal category, so lean on **editorial + creative galleries** more
than the repo's product-focused seeds. Borrow *typographic identity* from creative
references and *clean presentation* from product pages (Ghost, Mobbin) — combine into a
voice that's clearly the author's own.

## External galleries (inspiration, not sources to replicate)

- Awwwards (personal sites category), CSS Design Awards
- Godly / Minimal Gallery / SiteInspire — curated personal & portfolio work
- One Page Love — single-page portfolios
- Httpster — clean one-pagers

> Rule: portfolios should reveal the person. A portfolio that hides its author's voice is
> the anti-pattern.

## Synthesis guidance

- Lead with identity: a statement, a visual signature, a selection of the best work (curated
  over exhaustive).
- Case studies carry the persuasion — problem → approach → outcome → role, with real work.
- Editorial/expressive typography and asymmetric layout are strengths here, not risks.
- Make contact effortless: a clear ask, quick form or email, stated response time.
