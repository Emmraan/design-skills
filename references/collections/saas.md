# Collection: SaaS

The SaaS category is the largest and most competitive — most of the world's landing-page
templates are SaaS pages. This collection lists the strongest references in the repo for
building an **original** SaaS design, plus the external galleries worth mining for
inspiration.

## Category definition

Subscription software sold to teams or professionals. The page must sell an outcome, prove
it with product, and remove signup friction — see `patterns/saas-landing.md` for assembly.

## Repo references (analyzed in `references/websites/`)

| Site | Slug | What to borrow as logic |
|---|---|---|
| Ghost | `ghost` | Editorial, text-forward confidence; calm light theme; premium restraint |
| ClickUp | `clickup` | Bold/playful color; dense feature storytelling at scale; energetic CTA |

Use these three together: **layout** from one, **color logic** from another, **type pairing**
from the third — synthesize, never copy one.

## Synthesis guidance

- The default SaaS look (centered hero + purple gradient + 3-card pricing) is **exhausted** —
  change at least 2 of: hero layout, color strategy, pricing layout.
- Pick a non-purple primary; prefer tinted neutrals over pure gray.
- Lead with a different proof type than your competitor (outcome stat vs logo wall).
- Keep whitespace rhythm generous (96-128px sections) — SaaS lives on spacing.
