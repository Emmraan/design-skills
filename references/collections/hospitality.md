# Collection: Hospitality

Hospitality design sells **being taken care of**. The page must feel calm and
generous (space, imagery, human faces), prove reliability (team, locations,
reviews), and make contact effortless. Restraint reads as wealth; clutter reads
as risk.

## Category definition

Hotels, private aviation, concierge services, retreats-as-service, premium rentals.
Goal: desire (place, craft, calm) → trust (people, places, proof) → contact / book.
One contact CTA repeated beats feature grids.

## Synthesis guidance

- Lead with a giant serif statement and a single pill action.
- Split audiences into separate paths (private vs corporate) rather than shared bands.
- Pace cream with charcoal inversion bands for people/place proof.
- Keep body text AAA; relegate decorative grays to non-text roles only.
