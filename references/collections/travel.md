# Collection: Travel

Travel design sells **a place you are not yet in**. The page must make the
destination feel reachable (imagery, itineraries, guides) and booking feel safe
(price clarity, reviews, easy contact). Discovery inspires; proof converts.

## Category definition

Tour operators, retreats, stay marketplaces, travel agencies, destination guides.
Goal: inspire (place, experience) → trust (guides, reviews, transparent pricing) →
book / enquire. One booking CTA repeated beats many competing actions.

## Synthesis guidance

- Lead with a statement hero (place + promise) and a single booking CTA shape.
- Make listings the proof band; guides/hosts the trust band.
- Ration one warm accent for key moments — warmth reads as special only when rare.
- Keep body text AAA on dark; relegate low-contrast grays to large or decorative roles.
