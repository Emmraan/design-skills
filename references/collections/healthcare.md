# Collection: Healthcare

Healthcare design sells **trust and calm competence** — patients arrive anxious, buyers
(employers, plans) need proof at scale. The page must lower anxiety, prove outcomes, and
make the next step obvious. This collection maps the repo's healthcare references.

## Category definition

Virtual care, clinics, family health, and health benefits. Goal: empathy → credibility →
book / get pricing. Dual audiences (members + buyers) share one visual language with
separate proof bands.

## Synthesis guidance

- Lead with empathy in the hero (statement + human proof), follow with program taxonomy.
- Categorize with dots/eyebrows, never rainbow fills — more than ~6 categories becomes noise.
- Keep action color AA-or-better for its size: buttons/links only, never small body text.
- Prove twice: member stories for patients, outcome stats for buyers — separate bands, one language.
