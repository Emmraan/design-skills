# Collection: Agency

Agency design sells **craft and a point of view** — clients hire a team, so the page must
demonstrate skill, showcase the work, and build affinity. This collection maps the repo's
references that model agency-grade craft and the proof that earns work.

## Category definition

Design studios, dev agencies, creative firms, and consultancies. Goal: demonstrate craft →
build affinity → get a conversation. See `patterns/portfolio-agency.md` for assembly.

## Repo references (analyzed in `references/websites/`)

| Site | Slug | What to borrow as logic |
|---|---|---|
| Primora | `primora` | Craft-forward presentation; premium, editorial polish |
| KOTA | `kota` | Bold-brutalist type-led agency; single-family scale contrast, pill system, animated gradient moment |

Primora is the seed craft reference; pair it with **portfolio-agency galleries** and with
*product* references (Ghost, Mobbin) for editorial restraint and product-grade polish.
An agency page is judged on craft — borrow editorial typography, asymmetric layouts, and
case-study depth from the strongest creative references.

## External galleries (inspiration, not sources to replicate)

- Awwwards, CSS Design Awards — agency winners, case studies
- Godly / SiteInspire / Minimal Gallery — curation of creative sites
- One Page Love, Httpster — single-page agency/portfolio examples

> Rule: an agency page that looks templated fails its own pitch. Originality is the product.

## Synthesis guidance

- Lead with the work at the top; contact at the bottom; proof in between.
- Establish a point of view in the hero — a stance, not a generic tagline.
- De-risk hiring you: show a clear process and honest client outcomes.
- Editorial, asymmetric layouts are the *point* — this is where breaking the symmetric
  template is expected, not a bug.
